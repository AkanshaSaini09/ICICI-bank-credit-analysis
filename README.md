# ICICI Bank – Credit Risk & Financial Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive credit and financial analysis of **ICICI Bank**, evaluating its financial strength, liquidity position, capital adequacy, profitability, leverage, and asset quality over FY2024, FY2025, and FY2026.

The analysis applies key banking and credit-risk indicators to assess the bank's financial resilience, capacity to absorb potential losses, and overall creditworthiness. It combines financial data, year-on-year trend analysis, graphical visualisations, and qualitative interpretations to develop a lending recommendation.

The project was completed as part of the **Financial Risk Management** coursework at the Fortune Institute of International Business (FIIB).

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Evaluate ICICI Bank's profitability and earnings growth.
- Assess liquidity adequacy and the stability of its deposit base.
- Analyse capital adequacy and financial leverage.
- Examine asset quality through Gross NPA, Net NPA, and Provision Coverage Ratio.
- Compare key financial indicators across FY2024, FY2025, and FY2026.
- Identify the bank's financial strengths, potential risks, and areas requiring monitoring.
- Develop an overall credit assessment and a reasoned lending recommendation.

## Scope of Analysis

The project evaluates ICICI Bank across five major dimensions.

### 1. Profitability Analysis

Profitability analysis examines the bank's earnings performance and its ability to generate sustainable income.

**Key indicators:**
- Net Interest Income (NII)
- Operating Profit
- Profit Before Tax (PBT)
- Profit After Tax (PAT)
- Net Interest Margin (NIM)
- Return on Equity (ROE)

**Key findings:**

- Net Interest Income increased from ₹74,306 crore in FY2024 to ₹88,075 crore in FY2026.
- Profit After Tax increased from ₹40,888 crore to ₹50,147 crore over the same period.
- Operating profit increased from ₹58,122 crore to ₹70,401 crore.
- NIM declined from 4.53% to 4.32% and remained stable in FY2026.
- ROE moderated from 18.7% to 16.0%, indicating lower returns relative to shareholders' equity despite growth in absolute earnings.

**Interpretation:** The bank demonstrated consistent earnings growth, although the moderation in NIM and ROE represents an area for continued monitoring.

### 2. Liquidity Analysis

Liquidity analysis assesses the bank's ability to meet its short-term obligations and maintain adequate funding for its lending activities.

**Key indicators:**
- Cash and Savings Account (CASA) Ratio
- Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR)
- Total Deposits
- Total Advances
- Deposit growth versus advances growth

**Key findings:**

- Total deposits increased from ₹14,12,825 crore in FY2024 to ₹17,94,625 crore in FY2026.
- Total advances increased from ₹11,84,406 crore to ₹15,53,893 crore.
- LCR remained above 120% throughout the period, reaching 125.64% in FY2026.
- CASA declined from 40.4% to 38.9%.
- In FY2026, advances grew by 15.81%, compared with deposit growth of 11.44%.

**Interpretation:** ICICI Bank maintained comfortable liquidity and a growing deposit base. However, advances growing faster than deposits could increase funding pressure if the trend continues.

### 3. Capital Structure and Capital Adequacy

This section evaluates the bank's capital strength, leverage, and capacity to absorb unexpected losses.

**Key indicators:**
- Debt-to-Equity Ratio
- Total Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR)
- Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Ratio

**Key findings:**

| Indicator | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Debt-to-Equity | 0.21x | 0.21x | 0.19x |
| Total CAR | 16.33% | 16.55% | 17.18% |
| CET1 Ratio | 15.60% | 15.94% | 16.35% |

- Total CAR improved by 0.85 percentage points between FY2024 and FY2026.
- CET1 increased by 0.75 percentage points over the same period.
- The FY2026 CAR of 17.18% was above the 11.70% regulatory minimum used in the report.
- The FY2026 CET1 ratio of 16.35% was above the 8.20% regulatory minimum used in the report.
- The reported Debt-to-Equity Ratio declined to 0.19x in FY2026.

**Interpretation:** The reported indicators show strengthening capital buffers and lower leverage, supporting the bank's capacity to absorb financial shocks. Regulatory comparisons should be interpreted against the applicable requirements and reporting basis.

### 4. Credit Risk and Asset Quality

This section examines the quality of the bank's loan portfolio and the adequacy of provisions against potential credit losses.

**Key indicators:**
- Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA)
- Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA)
- Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR)

**Key findings:**

| Indicator | FY2024 | FY2025 | FY2026 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Gross NPA | 2.16% | 1.67% | 1.44% |
| Net NPA | 0.44% | 0.39% | 0.35% |
| Provision Coverage Ratio | 80.3% | 76.2% | 75.8% |

- GNPA declined by 0.72 percentage points between FY2024 and FY2026.
- NNPA declined by 0.09 percentage points over the same period.
- The decline in both NPA ratios indicates improvement in reported asset quality.
- PCR decreased from 80.3% to 75.8%, indicating a reduction in the proportion of NPAs covered by provisions.

**Interpretation:** Asset quality improved over the analysis period. However, the declining PCR should be monitored to assess whether provisioning remains adequate relative to potential credit losses.

### 5. Overall Credit Assessment

The final assessment consolidates the profitability, liquidity, capital adequacy, leverage, and asset-quality findings.

| Parameter | FY2026 Assessment |
|---|---|
| Profitability | Strong, with growing earnings |
| Liquidity | Comfortable, with LCR above 100% |
| Capital Adequacy | Strong, with improving capital ratios |
| Leverage | Comfortable based on the reported ratio |
| Asset Quality | Improving, with declining GNPA and NNPA |
| Provision Coverage | Monitoring required |

## Methodology

The project follows a comparative financial and credit-analysis approach.

1. **Data compilation:** Organising the reported financial indicators for FY2024, FY2025, and FY2026.
2. **Ratio analysis:** Evaluating profitability, liquidity, capital adequacy, leverage, and asset quality.
3. **Year-on-year comparison:** Identifying increases, decreases, and stable trends in key indicators.
4. **Growth calculations:** Calculating changes in earnings, deposits, advances, and other financial measures.
5. **Visualisation:** Presenting key trends through tables, bar charts, and line charts.
6. **Credit interpretation:** Assessing how the observed trends affect financial resilience and potential credit risk.
7. **Final recommendation:** Integrating the findings into an overall lending assessment and identifying areas requiring monitoring.

## Key Financial Insights

The analysis identifies the following major findings:

**Strengths**
- Consistent growth in Net Interest Income and Profit After Tax.
- Comfortable liquidity, with LCR remaining above 120%.
- Improving Total CAR and CET1 ratios.
- Declining Gross and Net NPA ratios.
- A growing deposit base and lower reported Debt-to-Equity Ratio.

**Risks and Monitoring Areas**
- NIM remains below its FY2024 level.
- ROE has moderated despite increasing profits.
- Advances grew faster than deposits in FY2026.
- CASA declined over the analysis period.
- Provision Coverage Ratio decreased across the three financial years.

## Final Lending Recommendation

Based on the indicators and interpretations presented in the report, the project arrives at a **STRONG / FAVOURABLE** overall credit assessment of ICICI Bank.

The recommendation is supported by consistent earnings growth, comfortable liquidity, strengthening capital adequacy, lower reported leverage, and improving asset quality.

However, continued monitoring is recommended for net interest margin, return on equity, the relative growth of advances and deposits, and provision coverage.

The recommendation represents an academic credit assessment based on the selected indicators; it is not a formal lending decision or an investment recommendation.
